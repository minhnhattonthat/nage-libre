                                About the game                              
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Five nymphs stray off into a different world "Nage," and fight in order to 
return to their former world. The fighting technique is a card battle, and 
only the high school girl's lovely offense and defense develops. The attack 
changes depending on the kind of extracurricular activities selected when the 
stage begins.          
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Cards
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 8 cards for the card battle are as follows
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ATK card (G100): to attack 
2. DEF card (G200): to defend 
3. HP card  (G300): to recover HP 
4. ESC card (G400): to escape from the card battle 
5. SP card  (G700): a super-killer shot 
6. CHG card       : to change card in battle (cannot bought)
7. COS card       : to change costume in battle (cannot be bought)
8. ??? card       : various action such as ATK, DEF or HP in battle (cannot 
                                                                   be bought)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Battle Command
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Stand by 
2. Attack
3. Item
3. Exchange 
3. Pocketbell
4. Anime
5. Save
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Note: the small square icons on the MAP are red (enemy), yellow (treasure 
chest) and light blue (nymph).
