Nage Libre: Seijaku no Suishin, English translation patch v{version}
=====================================================================

Original game: Nage Libre - Seijaku no Suishin (Varie, 1995, Super Famicom)

What is translated
------------------
- All story text: 2257 dialogue and narration entries in 333 scenes, plus the opening and the ending.
- System text: battle messages, names, clubs, birthstones, prompts and menus.
- The map command menu and the Pager's WHO window (graphics).
- The status screen labels and the "Rest?" prompt (graphics; not yet seen in play).

How to patch
------------
1. Get an unheadered ROM of the original game:
     size   1,572,864 bytes (1.5 MB)
     CRC32  {base_crc32}
     SHA-1  {base_sha1}
2. Apply NageLibre_EN_v{version}.bps with Flips (Floating IPS) or beat.
3. The patched ROM is 2 MB:
     CRC32  {patched_crc32}

The patch expands the ROM to 2 MB. Play it in an emulator or on a flash cart that supports 2 MB LoROM.
